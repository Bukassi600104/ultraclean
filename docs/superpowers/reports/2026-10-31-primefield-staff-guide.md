# Primefield Farm staff and CEO guide

This guide describes the prepared Pulse update. It is not evidence that production has been deployed.

## Bimbo: first setup

1. Open CEO Dashboard → Farm control centre. Choose a responsibility period and dates to review one manager, or Whole farm for the complete business.
2. Under Sites and named batches, name actual sites, ponds, flocks, pens or herds. New batches start empty. Allocate existing recorded animals; this organizes stock and does not buy or create animals.
3. In Manage Accounts, create the manager account. Open its records, select the operating period and Assign assets. Assign the actual batches, feed and supplies. New managers confirm the assigned physical inventory during onboarding; discrepancies remain visible for review.
4. Existing managers retain their established history and cash workflow. Starting a personal period is an explicit decision, never an automatic reset.
5. Configure only genuine values: approved credit days, bag weights, crate size, alert thresholds and optional dated currency display. Automatic purchase approval and external summaries remain off until configured.

## Managers: everyday work

1. Today: choose the assigned batch and actual activity date. Enter measured feed, deaths, eggs or sample weights where applicable. Leave unmeasured information unknown. Attach actual photos and send once.
2. Spending: submit purchase lines and quotes. Approval is permission, not payment. Record the amount actually paid and record delivered quantities separately; partial deliveries remain outstanding.
3. Funds: confirm money actually received. Money sent but not confirmed stays awaiting receipt. Spending above known funds remains pending Bimbo's funding decision; do not enter the same expense again.
4. Sales: select the real customer, quantities and price. Fish pricing uses weight while stock deduction uses fish count. An invoice records the sale; a receipt records actual money collected. Later payments do not deduct stock again.
5. Stock: record actual feed starting counts, dated bags received/opened and feeding separately. Opening a bag is not consuming all of it. Use physical counts to report discrepancies. Egg stock uses whole eggs; crates require a configured pack size.
6. Health: check due work and record actual completion with evidence. A vet's recommendation does not itself complete the manager's task.
7. More opens Funds, dedicated Stock/Mortality, reports and other existing workflows. Saved on phone and Upload pending are not confirmation that the server has received the entry. Keep queued conflicts for resolution; never change a closed-day entry's date just to send it.

## Bimbo: reconciliation and decisions

Farm control separates sales after credits, actual collections, paid operating costs, customer debt and tracked money held. Owner funding is not sales. Procurement handoffs move custody, not expenditure. Feed consumption estimates do not add a second expense.

Review purchases, unresolved funding, stock-count differences, overdue customer debt and health work from their source links. Corrections require a reason and retain the original record. For an actual commercial return on a paid invoice, record the credit first, then the actual refund against the receipt; never reverse a genuine receipt to simulate a refund. A refunded credit cannot be erased while its actual refund remains outstanding.

Use the complete-snapshot Excel export or Print / save PDF. The special historical manager export remains restricted to Farm Manager and Emmanuel Adeolu. A new manager never inherits those managers' old debt or performance.

## Optional support accounts

Create a Farm vet or Farm accountant in Manage Accounts, then explicitly assign limited access in Farm configuration. Vets see assigned health work and can append advice. Accountants read only the selected financial scope. Both begin with no data access and cannot change money, stock or managers' completed work. Suspend/reactivate or remove access through the same account controls; history remains.

## Final hands-on acceptance

Bimbo and managers will verify their actual phones, installation prompt, camera permissions, offline/reconnection behavior and everyday workflow after the approved release. Report the screen, date, action and visible error without sharing passwords. Do not create test transactions in the live farm.
